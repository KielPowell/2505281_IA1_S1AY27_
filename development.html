7 Cures for a Lean Purse

The seven cures for lack of money in Arkad’s case have revealed to me that the key to proper money management lies in forming consistent habits: saving some of the money I earn, budgeting, investing wisely, securing the savings, preparing for future demands, and increasing my earning capacity. It is also suggested that one should own his or her house. I can incorporate these principles in my student life through keeping track of the money I receive and earn, budgeting my education expenses, saving some money, and acquiring skills that might be useful for my future success. (Clason, “Seven Cures for a Lean Purse.”)


5 Laws of Gold

These five laws emphasize the importance of making savings consistently and investing wisely. In particular, Nomasir's experience serves as a reminder that I should never invest in what I do not know or fall for any unusual offers of quick returns. When there is money to be invested, I will learn about its mechanism and get sound advice. As a student, I will guard money needed for my tuition fees. (Clason, “The Five Laws of Gold.”)


My Biggest Takeaway

What really caught my attention is that having financial security is something that is accomplished through discipline and learning in time. I don’t have to wait for any lucky strikes and unexpected winnings; rather, I should start with small things, such as setting up a budget, putting aside money, and gaining skills. The lesson that I have taken from the reading is about being disciplined yet practical. Income plays an important role here.
